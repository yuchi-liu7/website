AI-Based Medical Triage System for Oculoplastics Referral

Current Research

Feb 2026 - Present Emory University

Leading the statistical analysis for a groundbreaking fine-tuned Large Language Model designed to assist in medical referral triage for oculoplastic cases. This project represents the first-of-its-kind application of LLMs in this specific medical domain.

Key Contributions

  • Performance Validation: Utilized Weighted Kappa and AUROC metrics to quantify AI performance, demonstrating “near expert level” diagnostic accuracy
  • Data Visualization: Created comprehensive visualizations in R including confusion matrices, error distribution analyses, and ROC curves to communicate complex AI performance to clinical stakeholders
  • Safety Assessment: Validated model generalizability and safety by maintaining 100% sensitivity in identifying high-priority emergencies
  • Statistical Rigor: Applied advanced biostatistical methods to ensure robust evaluation of the AI system’s clinical utility

Global Health Data Analysis: COVID-19 Pandemic Study

Graduate Project

Sep 2025 - Dec 2025 Emory University

Comprehensive analysis of global health data with a focus on COVID-19 pandemic trends, utilizing database design and advanced SQL queries to extract meaningful insights from over 1 million health records.

Key Achievements

  • Database Architecture: Designed a 4-table relational database with Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) to manage and normalize 1,000,000+ rows of global health data
  • Advanced Querying: Developed complex SQL queries using subqueries and left joins to analyze mortality and recovery trends across multiple countries
  • Epidemiological Insights: Identified that the 36-60 age group experienced the highest COVID-19 burden in 2020, with detailed analysis of treatment success rates
  • Comparative Analysis: Quantified that Hepatitis led all tracked diseases in mortality rate at 5.22%, providing context for pandemic response priorities

Technical Highlights

SQL Database Design Data Normalization ERD Epidemiology Public Health

Methodology

The project employed rigorous data management principles, including normalization to reduce redundancy and ensure data integrity. Complex analytical queries were optimized for performance while maintaining accuracy in cross-country comparisons.
